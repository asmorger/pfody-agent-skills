# A draft with no date

**Smell.** `(Draft: pending confirmation.)` `**pending client sign-off**`. `Needs Review`. All of them
correct about the state and silent about when — and often about who.

**Why it is wrong.** An undated marker cannot be closed. Nobody can tell whether it is a week old or
from the previous phase, so nobody chases it, and after a few months it becomes decoration: readers
learn that the markers mean nothing and start treating drafts as confirmed. The missing stakeholder
compounds it — "pending confirmation" with nobody named has no owner, so there is no one to ask. Both
omissions turn a live flag into permanent furniture.

**Instead.** Every marker carries a date, and a draft names the stakeholder it waits on: `(Draft:
pending operations lead confirmation as of 2026-11-08.)` A decided clause carries `(confirmed
2026-10-02)`. A supersession carries its date. Then an audit can list what has been pending too long,
which is the only reason the marker exists
([`../patterns/inline-status-markers.md`](../patterns/inline-status-markers.md),
[`../practices/audit-the-base.md`](../practices/audit-the-base.md)).
