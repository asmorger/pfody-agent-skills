# Resolving a conflict silently

**Smell.** The base states one of two conflicting positions and never mentions the other. Or a rule
quietly rewritten to match the newest source, with a fresh date and no change entry. Or both sources
weighed in prose — "the later discussion suggests…" — where neither carries an authority level.

**Why it is wrong.** Which statement holds is a question about authority and about what has happened
since, not about the statements, and a reader comparing two sentences has neither fact. A quietly
resolved conflict is the most expensive thing this base can carry because it looks settled: it is
cited by stories, built, and discovered by the stakeholder who said the other thing. No trail exists
either, so when the argument reopens it starts from zero.

**Instead.** Compare recorded authority levels; prefer the higher and say which. Write `CH-NNNN` with
both anchor sets, the previous understanding and the interpretation. Where authority does not settle
it — two level-3 clarifications, for instance — open a question and leave the rule's contested clause
marked pending. One entry and one question now
([`../principles/conflicts-are-recorded-not-resolved.md`](../principles/conflicts-are-recorded-not-resolved.md),
[`../patterns/authority-ladder.md`](../patterns/authority-ladder.md)).
