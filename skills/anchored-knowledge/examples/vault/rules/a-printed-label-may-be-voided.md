# LABEL-2 — A printed label may be voided

A printed label may be voided while its parcel has not been collected. A voided label is refused by
the carrier at collection, and the parcel returns to the unlabelled state.

> [!question] Open
> Who may void a printed label is unsettled — any clerk, or a supervisor. See [[who-may-void-a-printed-label|Q-0003]].
